Settled decisions

Blue border = new or changed in v2
V2 · MODELPTO Request holds employee, approver, status, reason. Leave Line Items (master-detail) hold date, category, hours.
V2 · APPROVALApprove or reject the whole request. Reject needs a reason, stored in Decision Notes.
V2 · RESUBMITAlways a new request. The rejected request stays as history; its dates are free again.
V2 · SYNC ERROROnly the failing day is flagged. The request stays Approved; HR can retry.
V2 · NOTESOne reason per request. No per-day notes.
V2 · UIPast and Approve: request list left, selected request's days right. Stacks on narrow screens. Managers and HR pick an employee in Past (HR retries failed days there).
V2 · APPROVAL SCREENA decision made in the standard approval screen is stamped too: who, when and the approver's comment.
V2 · HOLIDAYSDeferred. Flow and loader removed for now.
LIMITSMax hours per category from custom metadata. Daily max includes logged hours.
DUPLICATESSame date + category blocked; different category on the same day allowed.
ROLESHR Admin: everything. Manager: team heatmap, apply for and approve direct reports. User: apply for self.
DIRECT REPORTBoth Employee__r.Manager__c and the employee's User.ManagerId must point to the manager.
LATE APPROVALNo timesheet yet: lines wait and are converted when the timesheet is created.
APPROVAL PROCESSOptional, kept outside the package. In-tab decisions close its workitem.
WEEKENDS / BALANCEWeekends skipped by default. Over balance warns but allows.
CANCELCancelling approved leave is out of scope.
